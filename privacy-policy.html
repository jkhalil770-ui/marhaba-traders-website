Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Marhaba Traders through our onboarding forms or communication channels, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and LinkedIn profile.
  • Startup Information: Pitch decks, financial data, company registration details, product information, and strategic roadmaps.
  • Usage Data: Information on how you navigate and interact with our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the purpose of providing our services, which includes:

  • Evaluating your startup's investment readiness.
  • Creating financial models, pitch decks, and investor profiles.
  • Matching your startup with potential investors (only with your explicit consent).
  • Communicating updates, feedback, and deliverables regarding your project.

3. Data Confidentiality & Sharing

We understand that your business data and intellectual property are highly sensitive. We enforce strict confidentiality standards:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business information to any third parties.
  • Your pitch decks and financial data are only shared with verified investors within our network after we have received your approval to proceed with introductions.
  • Internal access to your data is restricted to Marhaba Traders consultants directly working on your project.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your documents and personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We utilize secure file hosting and encrypted communication channels for all sensitive transfers.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use standard cookies to improve user experience and analyze site traffic. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, though some features of our site may not function properly without them.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
